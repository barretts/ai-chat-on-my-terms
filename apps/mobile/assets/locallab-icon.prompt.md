# LocalLab app icon

Created with the built-in image generation tool. The opaque square PNG is used
by Expo as the app icon and web favicon; each platform generates its required sizes.

## Generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: production iOS home-screen app icon for LocalLab, an offline and private AI chat app. Generate ONE finished square icon, exactly 1024 by 1024 pixels, opaque PNG artwork. A single bold warm-white symbol merging a rounded speech bubble with a simple rounded microchip: the chat bubble has a small lower-left tail, and only two short thick chip pins per side, clean and readable at 48 pixels. Inside the bubble, two short offset violet rounded bars suggest conversation and subtly hint at the letters LL without literal text. Deep violet background aligned with Material purple #6750A4, with an extremely subtle tonal gradient; a tiny mint accent on one bar if it improves clarity. Center the symbol at about 62 percent of the canvas width with generous consistent margins. Premium restrained geometric design, optically balanced, smooth anti-aliased edges, flat graphic with very subtle depth at most. Full-bleed square background extending to all four edges; iOS will apply its own corner mask. No rendered phone, no home-screen mockup, no grid of alternatives, no surrounding border, no lettering, no caption, no watermark, no transparency.
