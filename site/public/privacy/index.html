Privacy policy

Effective October 19, 2026. This policy describes AI Chat on My Terms, developed by Barrett Sonntag, and this support website. The app is in release preparation; this policy must be reconciled with the final release before App Store submission.

Conversations and files on your device

The app stores conversations, settings, and imported content on your device. On-device model inference processes the content on the device. Downloading models or voice resources contacts the selected download host, which receives network information such as your IP address and the requested resource. Device or system backups may contain app data according to your backup settings.

Servers and third-party AI

If you choose a remote connection, content needed for the request is sent to that connection. Depending on the feature, this can include your current message, conversation context, system instructions, selected memories, attachments, images, or audio. Your own server and its operator can receive this content.

Supported cloud connections can include OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, Mistral, OpenRouter, or another endpoint you configure. The app must identify the destination and obtain permission before sharing personal content with third-party AI. Selecting a remote route means the request is processed outside your device. The receiving provider's own terms, retention policies, and privacy policy apply. We do not promise that a provider deletes requests immediately or never uses them for training.

Credentials

On iOS, provider credentials are stored using the device's secure credential storage and supplied to the selected endpoint to authenticate requests. Do not configure endpoints you do not trust. API keys and custom authentication headers should not be included in support messages or screenshots.

Microphone, speech, camera, and photos

The app requests access when a feature needs it. Selected photos and camera images can become chat attachments. Microphone input may be transcribed into text. Speech processing may run on-device or use a selected system or remote service, depending on your settings and available language support. Content attached to a remote request is shared with that destination.

Optional network features

Web search, remote tools, media generation, and companion connections may send request content and connection information to the endpoints you configure. Use only services you trust. An optional feature's presence in development does not mean a hosted service or account is included in the public release.

Diagnostics and support

Local trace recording is off by default. If you enable it, inspect logs before sharing them. When you email support, we receive the contact information, message, and files you send. We use that information to respond and investigate issues, and retain it as needed to handle the request and meet applicable obligations. Email providers process those messages. Contact us to request deletion of support information, subject to any required retention.

Website

This site does not include advertising, tracking scripts, analytics scripts, or forms. The hosting and network providers process connection information needed to deliver pages, such as IP addresses and request details, under their own policies. The website does not receive your app conversations.

Your choices and deletion

You can use supported on-device chat without configuring a cloud AI provider. Stop using or remove a remote connection to stop future requests to it; this does not erase records already held by that provider. Manage device permissions in iOS Settings. Delete conversations in the app and manage exports and backups separately. Uninstalling the app is not a promise that secure credentials, backups, exports, or provider records are removed.

Contact and changes

Contact Barrett Sonntag at barrett@sosuke.com for privacy questions. We will update this page when the app's data practices change.
