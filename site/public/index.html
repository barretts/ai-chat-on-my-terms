A chat app being prepared for iPhone and iPad. Choose on-device models, connect to your own model server, or configure a supported cloud provider with your own API key.

On-device models require a download and enough memory and storage. Remote providers require a connection and may charge separately. Availability and performance depend on the device, model, and provider.

Release status

The app is in release preparation. An App Store release date has not been announced.
